### This directory contains the data to process the results of the Nup62-Nup58-Nup54 complex condensate simulations.
Matlab scripts in each directory plot the output data for:
- Radius - radius of a condensate consisting of 32 copies of the Nup62-Nup58-Nup54 complex.
- surface_tension - surface tension of a condensate consisting of 32 copies of the Nup62-Nup58-Nup54 complex.
- Rg - comparison of the radius of gyration in a condensate compared to the dilute phase.
- contact_map - inter-Nup62-Nup58-Nup54 complex contact map in the condensate phase.
- OD_IDR - internal organization of the ordered domain (OD) and intrinsically disordered region (IDR) in condensates formed by the Nup62-Nup58-Nup54 complex.
